Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

BraxCode Digitals Foundation operates this site, including the Nexus AI assistant. This page explains what data we collect and how it is used.

Chat with Nexus

Your conversation history is saved only in your own browser (local storage on your device), not on our servers. Clearing the chat in Nexus, or clearing your browser data, removes it.

To generate a reply, each message you send passes through our server and is forwarded to our AI provider, Groq, for processing. When you continue an existing conversation, the most recent messages in it (up to the last 20) are sent along as context so the assistant can follow the thread. We do not keep your chat messages in our database.

Project briefs

When you confirm a project brief with Nexus, the summary (project type, scope, budget, timeline, and any contact details you provide) is saved to our database so Braxton can follow up with you. This is the only chat-related information we store.

Contacting us on WhatsApp or by email

The WhatsApp and email links on this site open those services directly. Anything you send there is held by WhatsApp or your email provider under their own privacy policies, not this one.

Cookies and analytics

This site sets no advertising or analytics cookies and runs no tracking scripts. We use Google Search Console only to see how our pages appear in Google search results; it reports aggregated search data to us and does not add tracking code to the site.

Project images

Images shown in the project gallery are stored so we can display our portfolio publicly. They are not linked to any visitor's personal data.

Your rights

You can ask us to review or delete any project brief information you have shared by reaching out on WhatsApp or emailing braxtonbruzzzy@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves. Continued use of the site means you accept the current version.
