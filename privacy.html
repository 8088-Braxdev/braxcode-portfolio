Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

BraxCode Digitals Foundation operates this site, including the Nexus AI assistant. This page explains what data we collect and how it's used.

Chat with Nexus

Your conversation with Nexus is stored only in your own browser (local storage on your device). We do not receive or store your chat messages on our servers, and Nexus does not have memory of past visits.

Project Briefs

When you confirm a project brief with Nexus, the summary (project type, scope, budget, timeline, and any contact details you provide) is saved to our database so Braxton can follow up with you. This is the only chat-related information we store.

Contacting us on WhatsApp

If you choose to continue the conversation on WhatsApp, that exchange is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy, not this one.

Analytics

We use Google Search Console to understand how the site performs in search results. It does not track individual visitors or their behavior on the site.

Project images

Images shown in our project gallery are stored to display our portfolio publicly and are not linked to any visitor's personal data.

Your rights

You can ask us to review or delete any project brief information you've shared by reaching out on WhatsApp or emailing braxtonbruzzzy@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves. Continued use of the site means you accept the current version.
